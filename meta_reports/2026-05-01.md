Every scene runs 30–50 % longer than its per-scene budget, but the
**aggregate** still matches: prompt asked for 60–90 s, script estimated
72 s, YouTube reports 73 s. So the model is honoring the global
constraint but ignoring the per-scene sub-budgets.

Mild concern: scene 1 opens "This is autodev. It's an autonomous
multi-agent system that watches AI and ML publications for trending
topics, selects one, decomposes it into milestones, and ships a working
codebase to GitHub through pull requests — at human pace..." That's a
40-word run-on after the four-word intro. For YouTube retention, the
first 5 s is normally where the steepest drop happens — a 12-word hook
before any technical detail would likely lift retention.

**Why I'm not editing on this yet:** with 1 view we have no retention
curve to confirm or refute. Editing the prompt now is acting on a
plausible theory rather than data. If the next 3–5 videos all show
sub-30 % retention at 5 s in YouTube Studio, that becomes the obvious
next-period edit (insert a "FIRST 5 SECONDS rule" into the screenplay
PROMPT).
